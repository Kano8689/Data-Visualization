# 📊 Power BI Business Intelligence Projects

This folder contains my **Power BI business intelligence and dashboard projects**, focused on transforming raw business data into interactive reports and actionable insights. These projects demonstrate practical experience with **Power Query for data transformation, DAX for calculations, data modeling, KPI development, interactive visualizations, and dashboard design** across sales, customer, product, and business performance analysis.

## 🚀 Projects

### 🍕 Pizza Sales Analysis

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze **pizza sales performance, revenue, orders, quantity sold, product categories, pizza sizes, and time-based sales trends**. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance and helps identify high-performing products and business trends.

🛠️ **Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX

📂 [View Project](./Pizza%20Sales)

### 🚗 Car Sales Analysis

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze **car sales performance, total revenue, cars sold, customers, dealers, brands, models, regions, customer demographics, and pricing metrics**. The dashboard provides business insights into sales performance and customer characteristics.

🛠️ **Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX

📂 [View Project](./Car%20Sales)

### 🌎 Global Super Store Sales Analysis

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze **global sales, revenue, profit, quantity sold, product categories, regions, customers, and order performance**. The project transforms transactional sales data into business intelligence through data modeling, Power Query, DAX, and interactive visualizations.

🛠️ **Tools:** Power BI, Power Query, DAX
